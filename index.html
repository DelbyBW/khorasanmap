FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Khorasan Map?

A map expansion for Euro Truck Simulator 2 that recreates North Khorasan in Iran, including its cities and main roads.

Which areas are included?

Shirvan, Esfarayen and Road 87 are currently built or under construction. See the coverage map above for the full picture.

Which game version and DLC do I need?

The supported game version and the required DLC will be confirmed with the first release. Check the download page for the details.

How do I install it?

Copy the .scs files into your ETS2 mod folder and enable them in Mod Manager. The download page has the full steps and the load order.
